Screamer Rally optional enhancements

The 16:9 camera patch and extended object/scenery admission patch are adapted
from rea-screamer-trilogy by AkitaOnRails and contributors (MIT option):
https://github.com/akitaonrails/rea-screamer-trilogy/blob/c9a21733d7b89cecd4646f1a5249fe482bb9c534/crates/screamer-launcher/src/fixes.rs

This implementation uses checked DOS executable patches; it does not include
Rick Barber's Windows runtime or renderer. The 16:9 presentation shader adapts
DOSBox Staging's sharp shader; its source retains the upstream SPDX notice.
DOSBox Staging's original license materials remain bundled with the runtime.
